## Working Frame

Busan Data Center Korea is being tracked as a Korea-based AI infrastructure and foreign-investment structuring initiative centered on a 100 MW Gangseo-gu data center project with pre-cleared power, a non-Seoul direct-power angle, and an incentive-led FDI package that could make Busan a flagship decentralization case.

## Verification Posture

- Treat all incentive percentages, tax-exemption ranges, cash-grant expectations, anchor-tenant assumptions, and project-cost placeholders in the memo as indicative rather than awarded.
- Treat claims about strategic fit, expected negotiation strength, and financing-program availability as memo-level positioning until corroborated by Korean authorities, counterparties, or binding paper.
- Preserve the memo as an architecture and negotiation map, not as proof that the package is already secured.
- Treat the internal Gate 1 approval as real internal workflow evidence for USG tracking, while still distinguishing internal approval from external counterparty verification or binding public-sector award.
