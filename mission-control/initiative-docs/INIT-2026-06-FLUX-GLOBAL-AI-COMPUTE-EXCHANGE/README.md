## Working Frame

Flux is being tracked as a global AI compute market-structure initiative: certified secondary-market GPU acquisition, trade-finance-backed throughput, and a DFI-supported reserve pool that prevents Global South buyers from being permanently priced out of legal compute access.

## Verification Posture

- The initiative record is intended to preserve the architecture, not to mark the counterparties as verified.
