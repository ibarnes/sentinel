# UnifiedAfrica Marketplace — Work Requirement (v0)

## Core mechanic
A project is only accepted once it has at least one verified commitment beyond the sponsor.

## Canonical flow
Submit → Screen → Conditional Approval → Coalition Commitment → Payment → Activation

## Plain-language rule
A project is not approved just because the sponsor wants help. A project becomes approved only when at least one other credible participant commits to the opportunity.

## Qualified participant examples
- Capital partner
- Operator
- Technology partner
- Anchor customer
- Government body
- Landowner
- Strategic buyer
- Distributor
- Family office
- Institutional partner

## Payment trigger
Once a project is conditionally approved and at least one qualified participant commitment is verified, the sponsor pays the activation fee.

## Positioning line
No project enters the marketplace on ambition alone. Every approved project must show outside commitment.

## Website copy (draft)
### Submit Your Project
UnifiedAfrica reviews serious African projects seeking capital, technology, operators, or strategic partners.

You can submit your project for review at no cost.
If your project passes our initial screen, it becomes conditionally approved.

A project is not fully approved until at least one qualified participant commits to engage with it. This may be a capital partner, technology partner, operator, strategic partner, government body, anchor customer, or other credible participant.

Once that commitment is verified, you pay the activation fee and your project enters the UnifiedAfrica marketplace.

This keeps the marketplace serious, protects partners from unready opportunities, and helps project sponsors prove there is real commitment around the deal.
